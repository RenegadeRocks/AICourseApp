# Week 2 lesson briefs — Personal branding & niche discovery

Week identity: Sessions "Importance of Personal Branding" (2026-05-30) + "Niche Discovery" (2026-05-31). Study window 2026-05-25 to 2026-05-31.

Week thesis: Block 0 + Week 1 of Block 1 gave you the capability and the sales mechanics. Week 2 closes the loop on **why any of this scales**: brand as the trust substitute in an asymmetric-information market, and niche as the compounding asset that makes brand legible. This is not "build an audience" content — it is the commercial logic of *reputation-as-distribution* and *niche-as-hypothesis*, at the same L3 depth as the technical weeks.

- **Title direction:** "Why brand matters for AI consultants — asymmetric trust, decision latency, and the premium-positioning floor"
- **Topic:** The economic case for personal brand in an AI-services market. Akerlof-style lemons-market analysis applied to AI consulting (buyers cannot distinguish good from bad).
