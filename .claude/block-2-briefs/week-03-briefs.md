# Week 3 lesson briefs — Building elegant landing pages & micro-prototypes

Week identity: Sessions "Building Elegant Landing Pages" (2026-06-06) + "How to Build Micro Prototypes" (2026-06-07). Study window 2026-06-01 to 2026-06-07.

Week thesis: The first AI worker a reader usually ships is a **conversion surface** — a landing page, a micro-prototype, or both — and the competitive advantage is no longer "can you build one" (v0/Lovable/Bolt/Replit make that a commodity in 2026) but "can you build one that actually converts and validates a hypothesis in 4-8 hours." This week is the mechanics of both: (1) what makes a landing page convert (not "make it pretty" — the copy hierarchy, the proof stack, the eye-flow, the measurable-benchmark math), (2) how modern AI-code-gen tools actually work under the hood and where they fail, (3) design-system literacy so you can override the AI's taste defaults, (4) the pretotyping → smoke-test → fake-door → concierge → MVP ladder, (5) the 4-hour shippable-prototype pipeline (Claude Code + v0/Lovable + Figma Make + instrumentation), (6) validation instrumentation that actually measures what a prototype is supposed to measure.

- Block 0 Week 3 covered problem discovery (JTBD) and AI fit scoring — do NOT re-teach. Reference in one line.
- Block 1 Week 1 covered commercial SOW and project phasing — do NOT re-teach. Week 3 is the BUILD layer.
- Block 1 Week 2 covered positioning — reference only when it bears on copy. Do not re-teach positioning theory.
