Week identity: Sessions "AI That Reads Your Business: Reports & Insights" (2026-06-20) + "Build: Weekly Business Report Generator" (2026-06-21). Study window 2026-06-15 to 2026-06-21.

Week thesis: The back-office analyst is the third production AI worker and arguably the one that creates the cleanest ROI story for a client pitch (turn 2 hours of analyst labor into 15 minutes). This week builds the document-understanding + data-connectivity + analytical-reasoning + structured-generation stack that sits behind "AI that reads your P&L / operations dashboard / ticket queue and writes the Monday report." The L3 angle isn't "use ChatGPT to summarize a PDF" — it's the mechanics of document extraction at accuracy targets that survive audit, the data-connectivity layer (MCP servers, read-only tokens, governance), the numerical-reasoning ceiling of current LLMs (FinQA, TabLLM, MATH-Hard benchmarks + code-execution offloading), and the evaluator-critic architectures that catch confidently-wrong reports before they ship to a CEO.

**Block 0 / Block 1 / Block 2 overlap to AVOID:**
- Block 0 covered LLM fundamentals, prompting, Claude Code — reference only.
- Block 1 covered commercial layer — reference only.
- Week 3 (Block 2) covered landing pages / prototypes — separate concern.
- Week 4 (Block 2) covered RAG and agent architectures in depth. This week references Week 4 patterns when the report generator needs retrieval (e.g., retrieving prior-period docs for comparison), but DOES NOT re-teach tool-calling loops, chunking, hybrid search, reranking, or evals. Build on top of Week 4.
