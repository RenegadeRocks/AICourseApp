# Week 9 briefs — Packaging & Selling Your AI Agents + First Sellable Agent Package

Curriculum sessions: "Packaging & Selling Your AI Agents" + "Create: Your
First Sellable Agent Package". July-2026 interpretation: turn a custom agent
build (the reader has Weeks 4–8 builds) into a *productized package* — fixed
scope, fixed price, repeatable delivery — and learn the 2026 distribution
surfaces for agent products. Block 1 taught selling custom services; this week
teaches the productized-service → product spectrum. Wikilink b1w01 for
pricing/SOW fundamentals; do not re-teach.

Day plan (researcher may sharpen; keep the arc):

- **01-mon — From build to package: the productization spectrum.** Custom
  service → productized service → product → platform; where agent businesses
  actually sit in 2026 (verify real examples with revenue evidence); scope
  collapse as THE productization move; what packaging changes about margin,
  delivery, and support. The "agency vs product" fork every builder faces.
- **02-tue — Package design: scope, tiers, guarantees.** Designing the
  package: outcome definition, inclusion/exclusion lists, tier ladders
  (starter/standard/enterprise), onboarding-as-part-of-product, SLAs and
  guarantees for probabilistic systems (how do you guarantee an agent?), the
  eval-report-as-deliverable move (link b2w04 eval discipline). Usage-based vs
  flat pricing for agent products in 2026 (verify current norms).
- **03-wed — Delivery engineering: one build, many customers.** Multi-tenant
  vs single-tenant agent deployments, config-over-code customization,
  templatized MCP/tool credentials per client, white-labeling, hosting
  economics with current model pricing, maintenance contracts (model
  deprecations as a revenue line — the 2026 Anthropic deprecation wave as
  evidence). Link b3w08 reliability for the unattended parts.
- **04-thu — Distribution surfaces for agent products in 2026.** Where agent
  products get discovered/sold now: marketplaces (verify current state: GPT
  Store-class, Claude ecosystem/skills, n8n templates, WhatsApp/Slack
  directories, vertical marketplaces), direct/outbound, partnerships/resellers,
  app-store take rates and their economics. What actually drives installs vs
  vanity listings (find evidence, not vibes).
- **05-fri — Pricing the package.** Value-metric selection for agents (per
  seat, per resolution, per run, per outcome), price anchoring against the
  labor line-item, the Artisan $250/mo self-serve collapse and what it means
  for entry pricing (b2w04 canonical — wikilink + build on it), discounting
  discipline, grandfathering through model-price changes (two-directional risk
  — b1w01 canonical). Worked pricing model with current COGS math.
- **06-sat — BUILD: your first sellable agent package.** Take one existing
  build (Week 4/5/8) and productize it end-to-end: package one-pager, tier
  sheet, onboarding checklist, delivery runbook, eval-report template, pricing
  model spreadsheet-equivalent (code-lab: a pricing calculator + package-spec
  generator the reader fills in), and a 5-minute demo script. Pass bar:
  a complete package a stranger could buy.
- **07-sun — Synthesis + quiz + flashcards.**

Controversies (verify current state): productized agents vs "every deployment
is a snowflake"; guarantees/SLAs on probabilistic systems (positions);
marketplace distribution hope vs direct-sales reality; usage-based backlash.
