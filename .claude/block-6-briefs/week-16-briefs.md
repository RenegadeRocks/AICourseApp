# Week 16 briefs — Monetization Paths + AI in Sales Calls + Pricing/Revenue Planning (Tiers & Upsell)

Curriculum sessions: "Explore monetisation paths: Using AI in Sales Calls &
Pricing Strategy" + "Pricing & Revenue Planning: Pricing tiers & upsell hooks".
July-2026 interpretation: turning the launched product into durable revenue.
b4w09 taught packaging + value-metric pricing THE PACKAGE (wikilink, don't
re-teach the fundamentals); this week goes deeper into monetization-model
selection, AI-assisted sales, and revenue architecture (tiers, expansion,
upsell). Ground pricing in the current model-cost reality (COGS from the July
refresh) so margins are real.

Day plan:

- **01-mon — Monetization models for AI products in 2026.** The menu:
  subscription, usage/credits, per-outcome, seat, hybrid, freemium, usage-
  based-with-floor; how AI COGS (token costs, the tokenizer change) reshape
  which models are viable; the 2026 usage-vs-subscription debate resolved with
  current evidence (b0w03 thesis + b4w09 — wikilink). Margin math with verified
  current pricing. Controversy: outcome-based pricing hype vs operational
  reality.
- **02-tue — Pricing strategy: value metric, anchoring, packaging.** Choosing
  the value metric that scales with customer value (not your cost), price
  points and psychological anchoring, good-better-best design, the "pricing is
  positioning" principle, discounting discipline, price testing with tiny N
  (Wilson discipline — b2w03 wikilink). Verify current comparable-product
  pricing in the reader's likely categories.
- **03-wed — AI in the sales motion.** Using AI across the sales cycle
  (research, personalization at draft-not-blast, call prep, real-time call
  assist, follow-up, CRM hygiene) — build on the b2w04 sales-agent tech
  (wikilink), here from the SELLER's workflow angle; the 2026 sales-tool
  landscape (verify current tools); call recording/consent law (b3w07
  canonical wikilink); where AI helps vs where it erodes trust in a human sale.
- **04-thu — Tiers, upsell hooks, and expansion revenue.** Designing the tier
  ladder for natural upgrade paths, usage-based expansion, the upsell/cross-
  sell moments (in-product upgrade prompts done tastefully — b5w13 magic
  wikilink), net revenue retention as the compounding metric (b5w14 wikilink),
  gating features by tier without crippling the free/entry experience.
  Controversy: aggressive upsell vs product-led expansion.
- **05-fri — Revenue planning & unit economics.** The model every founder
  needs: CAC, LTV, payback period, gross margin under AI COGS, the SaaS
  metrics that matter at each stage, churn's compounding damage, a simple
  revenue model the reader can actually maintain; when to raise price vs cut
  cost vs improve retention. Verify current benchmark ranges (hedge as
  directional). Pass-bar-able.
- **06-sat — BUILD: the monetization + revenue model.** For the reader's
  product: choose and justify a monetization model, design a 3-tier ladder
  with upsell hooks, build a unit-economics + revenue-projection model, and an
  AI-assisted sales-call prep + follow-up workflow. code-lab: a pricing/
  unit-economics calculator (CAC/LTV/payback/margin under token COGS) +
  tier-designer, compile-checked with a stress case. Pass bar: a defensible
  price and a model that shows the path to profitability.
- **07-sun — Synthesis + quiz + flashcards.**

Controversies (verify current): outcome-based pricing; freemium vs free-trial
vs paid-only for AI products; AI in human sales (assist vs erode trust);
NRR/expansion as the real growth engine.
