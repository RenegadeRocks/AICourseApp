# Week 18 briefs — Lead Magnet + Social Distribution + Paid Ads Lead Acquisition

Curriculum sessions: "Create a Lead Magnet using social media distribution" +
"Acquire leads using Paid ads". July-2026 interpretation: building the
demand-capture machine — a lead magnet that earns the opt-in, organic
distribution, and paid acquisition that doesn't set money on fire. b6w15 taught
the launch motion + earned distribution (wikilink); b1w02 taught audience/
branding (wikilink). This week is the repeatable lead-gen engine.

Day plan (researcher may sharpen; keep the arc):

- **01-mon — Lead magnets that actually convert in 2026.** The lead-magnet
  spectrum (guide/tool/template/mini-course/free-tier-of-the-product), why most
  lead magnets fail (no specific promise, no next step), the AI-native lead
  magnet (an actual working mini-tool/agent as the magnet — build on the
  reader's product), the opt-in value exchange in a spam-saturated inbox.
  Controversy: gated lead magnets vs give-everything-away (named positions).
- **02-tue — The opt-in funnel & list hygiene.** Landing/opt-in page (b4w10
  wikilink for page mechanics), lead capture → nurture → offer, email
  deliverability in 2026 (the b6w15 deliverability reality — wikilink),
  double-opt-in and consent (GDPR/CAN-SPAM), list segmentation, the welcome
  sequence, measuring opt-in rate not vanity subscribers. Verify current
  deliverability/ESP landscape.
- **03-wed — Organic social distribution engine.** Turning the lead magnet into
  a distribution flywheel: content→magnet→list loop, platform-native formats
  (LinkedIn/X/YouTube/short-form — the 2026 algorithm reality from b1w02,
  wikilink), repurposing one idea across platforms, the build-in-public tie-in
  (b6w15), authenticity in the anti-slop era. Verify current platform reach
  norms.
- **04-thu — Paid ads: the honest primer.** When paid makes sense (only with a
  working funnel + unit economics — b6w16 CAC/LTV/payback wikilink), the 2026
  ad-platform landscape (Meta Advantage+, Google, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, Reddit —
  verify current state + AI-driven campaign automation), creative that
  converts, audience targeting post-privacy-changes (b6w17 wikilink), the
  minimum-viable-test budget, why most beginners lose money on ads.
- **05-fri — Ad campaign mechanics & measurement.** Campaign structure,
  creative testing (small-N discipline — b2w03 Wilson wikilink), the metrics
  that matter (CAC, CPL, ROAS, payback — not CTR vanity), attribution reality
  in 2026 (b4w10 instrumentation wikilink), scaling a winner vs killing a
  loser, the AI-ad-tools landscape (creative gen, campaign optimization —
  verify current). Pass-bar-able.
- **06-sat — BUILD: the lead-gen engine + a paid test.** For the reader's
  product: design + build one AI-native lead magnet, an opt-in page + welcome
  sequence, a 2-week organic distribution plan, and a minimum-viable paid-ads
  test with a pre-registered kill/scale threshold and CAC target. code-lab: a
  lead-magnet/funnel-metrics calculator (opt-in rate, CPL, CAC, funnel
  conversion, paid-test decision rule) + a distribution-content planner,
  compile-checked. Pass bar: a lead engine with a measurable opt-in and a
  disciplined paid test.
- **07-sun — Synthesis + quiz + flashcards.**

Controversies (verify current): gated vs ungated lead magnets; paid ads for
indie/bootstrapped founders (worth it vs money-pit); organic-first vs paid-first
for a new product; AI-generated ad creative performance vs authenticity.
