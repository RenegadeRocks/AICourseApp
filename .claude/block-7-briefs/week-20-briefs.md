# Week 20 briefs — SOPs for Onboarding/Delivery/Growth + Build Community & Paid Inner Circle

Curriculum sessions: "SOPs for onboarding delivery & growth" + "Build Community
& paid inner circle". July-2026 interpretation: systematizing the business so it
runs without the founder in every loop, and building the community/paid-
membership layer that compounds retention and revenue. b4w09 packaging + b7w19
productized-service (wikilink) feed the SOP work; b6w17 growth loops + community
(wikilink) feed the community work. Closes Block 7.

Day plan:

- **01-mon — SOPs: the operating system of a business that scales.** What an
  SOP actually is (and isn't), the SOP hierarchy (onboarding/delivery/growth/
  support), writing SOPs that get followed (checklist > prose), the AI angle
  (AI-generated + AI-executed SOPs, agents running the routine parts — b3w08
  automation wikilink), versioning and ownership, when NOT to systematize.
  Controversy: heavy process vs founder-judgment at small scale.
- **02-tue — Onboarding SOPs: the first 30 days decide retention.** Client/
  customer onboarding as the highest-leverage SOP (activation drives retention
  — b5w14 wikilink), the onboarding checklist, time-to-first-value, automating
  onboarding (welcome sequences, setup automation, the client dashboard from
  b7w19 wikilink), reducing onboarding friction, measuring onboarding success.
  Verify current onboarding-benchmark evidence.
- **03-wed — Delivery & growth SOPs.** Delivery SOPs (the productized-service
  runbook from b7w19 — wikilink), quality gates (eval discipline b2w04
  wikilink), the growth SOP library (content, outreach, referral — b6w17
  wikilink), handoff-ready documentation, the SOP-to-automation pipeline (which
  SOPs become agents), maintaining SOPs as the business changes.
- **04-thu — Building community: the compounding moat.** Why community is the
  durable moat in the AI era (product features commoditize, belonging doesn't —
  build on b5w13 + b6w17, wikilink), free vs paid community, platform choice
  (Discord/Circle/Slack/Skool — verify current landscape + pricing), the
  cold-start problem for communities (the b6w17 loop mechanics wikilink),
  seeding culture, moderation, the founder's role. Controversy: free community
  as funnel vs paid community as product.
- **05-fri — The paid inner circle & membership economics.** Designing a paid
  community/membership/mastermind (the inner-circle model), pricing and tier
  design (b6w16 wikilink), member retention as the whole game (churn kills
  memberships — b5w14 wikilink), delivering ongoing value, the events/access/
  content mix, membership unit economics, avoiding the ghost-town death. Verify
  current membership-platform + pricing landscape.
- **06-sat — BUILD: SOP library + community launch plan.** For the reader's
  business: write 3 core SOPs (onboarding, delivery, one growth), identify
  which become agents, and a complete paid-community launch plan (platform,
  pricing/tiers, seeding, first-30-members plan, retention mechanic). code-lab:
  an SOP-template generator + a membership unit-economics/retention model
  (MRR, churn, LTV, the ghost-town early-warning metric), compile-checked.
  Pass bar: a runnable SOP library and a community a stranger could join with a
  clear value promise.
- **07-sun — Synthesis + quiz + flashcards + Block 7 capstone recap** (lead-gen
  → delivery infra → SOPs + community: the systematized business).

Controversies (verify current): process vs judgment at small scale; free vs
paid community; the 2026 course/community-platform shakeout (Skool etc.);
membership as durable revenue vs churn treadmill.
