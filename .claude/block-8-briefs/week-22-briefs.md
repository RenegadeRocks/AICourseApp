# Week 22 briefs — Final Thoughts, Recap & Checklists (the PROGRAM capstone)

Curriculum sessions: "Final Thoughts, Recap & Checklists - 1" + "Final Thoughts,
Recap & Checklists - 2". July-2026 interpretation: the graduation week. This is
the WHOLE-COURSE capstone — a rigorous, non-fluffy recap of all 8 blocks, master
checklists the graduate will actually use, the durable principles that outlast
any specific tool, and the forward path. It must be synthesis of the highest
order: dense wikilink integration across the entire vault, zero re-teaching,
zero motivational padding. Earn every sentence. This is also the week where the
course's own "distrust anything older than a quarter / re-verify" ethos gets
handed to the graduate as a permanent practice.

Day plan (researcher may sharpen; keep the arc — this week is recap-heavy so
lessons can be checklist/framework-dense rather than net-new-fact-dense):

- **01-mon — The whole map: what you actually learned.** A rigorous walk of the
  8-block arc as ONE integrated capability (not a list of topics), the
  through-lines that ran the whole course (evidence-over-vibes, unit-economics
  discipline, anti-slop, eval-gating, the canonical-homes/no-re-teaching
  discipline as a life skill), what compounds. Dense wikilinks. Reviewer:
  Seibel, Mollick, Karpathy.
- **02-tue — The master build checklist.** The definitive end-to-end checklist
  to take an idea → validated → built → launched → monetized → grown →
  systematized, each item wikilinked to the week that teaches it. The "have you
  actually done this?" audit for the graduate's own business. Pass-bar-able:
  the reader scores their own business against it.
- **03-wed — The durable principles (what outlasts the tools).** The ~10–15
  first-principles that survive model/tool churn: the ones the July-2026 refresh
  itself proved (models change monthly, principles don't), margin under real
  COGS, retention-first, evidence-driven decisions, the trifecta/security
  posture, human-in-the-loop where it matters. Each principle tied to where the
  course demonstrated it. Controversy: which "best practices" are actually
  durable vs fashion (named positions).
- **04-thu — Staying current: the graduate's re-verification practice.** The
  meta-skill the course was secretly teaching: how to keep this knowledge alive
  as the field moves. The `_last_verified` habit, reading model specs
  critically (b0w0 wikilink), the refresh discipline (this very course was
  refreshed in July 2026 — teach that as the example), building a personal
  intelligence system, knowing what to trust. What in THIS course will age
  fastest and how to tell.
- **05-fri — The forward path: your next 90 days.** A concrete 90-day plan
  template the graduate fills in based on their stage (idea / building /
  launched / scaling), the highest-leverage next action per stage, avoiding
  the tutorial-hell/perpetual-learner trap, accountability structures (the
  community b7w20 wikilink), when to hire/partner/raise, the honest
  probabilities. No hype — realistic paths.
- **06-sat — BUILD: your personalized graduation artifact.** The capstone
  deliverable: the graduate assembles their own (1) completed master-checklist
  audit with a score, (2) a 90-day plan, (3) their durable-principles one-pager,
  and (4) their re-verification system. code-lab: a graduation-audit tool
  (checklist scorer + 90-day-plan generator + a "what to re-verify and when"
  scheduler), compile-checked. Pass bar: a graduate walks away with a dated,
  personalized action plan and a self-audit score.
- **07-sun — PROGRAM CAPSTONE: synthesis + final quiz + flashcards + send-off.**
  The whole-program synthesis, a comprehensive final quiz drawing across all 8
  blocks (15 Q + answer key), a flashcard set of the durable principles, and a
  short, earned closing — not a motivational speech, but a clear statement of
  what the graduate can now do and the standard they should hold. This is the
  last page of the book; make it land honestly.

Controversies (verify where fact-bearing): agency vs product as the better
2026 path; which current tools/practices will age fastest; the realistic
success-probability conversation (honest, not doom or hype).
