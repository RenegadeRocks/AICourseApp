- **[01-mon:Layer 2 + Layer 4] MCP spec coverage stops at 2025-06-18.** The lesson mentions "2025-11-25" only in passing in [^14]'s revision list and never covers it. Evidence: https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/changelog and https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28-release-candidate/ .
