## Verdict

This is a strong week structurally — the six-stage pipeline frame, the failure taxonomies, and the eval discipline are durable and mostly still correct. But it was written into an "Opus 4.7 is the frontier" world that ended on June 9, 2026 (Claude Fable 5 / Mythos 5; Opus 4.8 and Sonnet 5 also now exist), and its two most operational recommendations have rotted badly: (1) the Wednesday lesson's first-pick connector — the reference Postgres MCP server — was deprecated/archived July 10, 2025 and carries a documented SQL-injection vulnerability, and (2) the Friday lesson teaches Anthropic structured outputs as a beta with a now-deprecated header, when the feature is GA with a changed API surface. "month end closer" and "general ledger reconciler", May 5, 2026), which undercuts Monday's "category 4 has no incumbent" commercial thesis, and misses the strongest adoption evidence in the replacement-vs-augmentation debate (Brynjolfsson/Chandar/Chen "Canaries" 13%→16% entry-level decline + live ADP dashboard). One April P0 from `_review.md` (the fabricated McCardel quote in 03-wed) was never fixed.
