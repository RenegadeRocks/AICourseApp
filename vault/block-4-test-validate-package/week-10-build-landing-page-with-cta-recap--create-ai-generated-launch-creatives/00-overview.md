# Week 10 — The launch surface: agent-product landing pages and AI-generated launch creatives

## The thesis of this week

Week 9 gave your agent a package: a name, a scope, a price. Week 10 gives it a public face, and the face has a harder job than any SaaS landing page you studied in [[01-mon-landing-page-as-conversion-machine|Block 2 Week 3]], because you are selling **probabilistic software to buyers who have already been burned by it**. The 2026 buyer arrives at your page asking three questions no 2020 SaaS page had to answer above the fold: *will it hallucinate in front of my customers, what happens to my data, and how do I know your demo isn't the best run you ever got?* Every section this week exists to answer those questions with evidence instead of adjectives.

The second half of the week is production: the July-2026 AI creative stack (image, video, voice, copy) can produce a full launch kit in a weekend for under $100, and the same stack has produced enough slop that consumers now punish brands for visibly using it. You will learn to use the stack for volume and keep humans on judgment, because the meta-signal is brutal: if your marketing looks like unreviewed AI output, buyers infer your agent is unreviewed AI output.

This is not a re-run of Week 3's landing-page material. Anatomy, CRO benchmarks, design-system literacy, and the statistics of small samples are canonical there; this week recaps them in one table and spends its budget on what is new: agent-specific conversion, the 2026 creative stack with verified pricing and commercial terms, and launch instrumentation that feeds Week 11's validation decision.

## Who this week is for

You have a Week 9 package from [[06-sat-build-your-first-sellable-agent-package|Week 9's build]] (or a stand-in: any agent you built in Blocks 2–3 with a scope and a price). You can already build a page with AI codegen tools ([[05-fri-prototype-pipeline]]), read a design system ([[03-wed-design-system-literacy]]), and run an eval harness ([[06-sat-rag-evaluation]]).

## Why these topics belong together

The week is one artifact seen from five angles. Monday writes the argument (copy that survives a skeptical 2026 buyer). Tuesday assembles the argument into a page with a deliberately chosen CTA. Wednesday picks the tools that will render the argument visually without triggering the slop reflex. Thursday runs the production line with QA gates and legal rails. Friday wires the whole thing to instruments so launch day produces a decision, not a dopamine spike. Saturday you ship it. The through-line is the same discipline Block 4 runs on: **claims require evidence, and launches are experiments with pre-registered outcomes**, which is exactly where Week 11 picks up.

## The one thing to internalize

A landing page for an agent product is an eval report wearing a suit. The trust gap is the conversion bottleneck, and the only durable way across it is measured, scoped, honestly-caveated proof. Everything else this week (creative, CTAs, instrumentation) is amplification on top of that.
