# Week 17 — Make growth compound after the launch

You launched (Week 15). You built pricing and monetization paths (Week 16).
Both are one-time events. A launch is a spike. A price is a number. Neither
compounds on its own. This week is about the machinery that keeps working while
you sleep: the feedback loop that turns user signal into the right roadmap, the
re-engagement system that wins back the users who drifted, and the growth loops
that make each new user help produce the next one.

This is the last content week of Block 6, so it is also where launch, monetize,
and grow finally connect into one go-to-market motion. By Saturday you will have
built a growth system for your own product. By Sunday you will have the whole
Block 6 arc in your hands.

## The through-line

Block 5 taught you steady-state product analytics: retention curves, the
north-star metric, the six AI-specific numbers that predict churn
([[block-5-product-building-principles/week-14-analytics-iteration-what-to-measure--scale-infra-auth-db-ui-polish/01-mon-product-analytics-for-ai-products|Week 14 analytics]]).
Block 4 taught launch-day instrumentation, the spike and the day-one funnel
([[block-4-test-validate-package/week-10-build-landing-page-with-cta-recap--create-ai-generated-launch-creatives/05-fri-launch-day-instrumentation|Week 10 instrumentation]]),
and the validation discipline that keeps you honest
([[block-4-test-validate-package/week-11-define-your-product-idea-validate-idea-using-ai--market-user-validation-interview-or-poll-potential-users/03-wed-interviews-that-dont-lie-to-you|Week 11 validation]]).
We do not re-teach any of that. This week is the growth-engineering layer that
sits on top: not what to measure, but what to build so the measurements go up
for reasons you can defend.

## The seven days

- **[[01-mon-the-feedback-engine|Mon]] · The feedback engine.** From raw signal
  to a prioritized roadmap. In-product surveys, the Sean Ellis 40% PMF test done
  right, support-as-signal, review mining, and closing the loop without building
  for the loudest user.
- **[[02-tue-retargeting-and-re-engagement|Tue]] · Retargeting & re-engagement.**
  Lifecycle segmentation, trigger-based win-back, the post-cookie retargeting
  reality of 2026, reactivation economics, and the ethics line between
  re-engagement and dark-pattern nagging.
- **[[03-wed-growth-loops-vs-funnels|Wed]] · Growth loops vs funnels.** Why loops
  compound and funnels leak. Content, viral, paid, and product loops. The
  AI-specific output-as-marketing loop. Instrumenting loop efficiency.
- **[[04-thu-referral-and-virality-engineering|Thu]] · Referral & virality.**
  Referral program design, k-factor and cycle-time math, when referral is real
  growth versus a growth-team fantasy, and fraud guards.
- **[[05-fri-the-growth-system-and-honest-measurement|Fri]] · The growth system.**
  Loops plus lifecycle plus feedback into one system. The input-metric tree,
  growth theater, and the retention-first principle.
- **[[06-sat-build-the-growth-system|Sat]] · BUILD.** A feedback loop, a
  re-engagement sequence, one instrumented growth loop, and a referral mechanic
  with honest k-factor math. Ships with a `code-lab/` growth-metrics simulator.
- **[[07-sun-synthesis-quiz-flashcards|Sun]] · Synthesis + Block 6 capstone.**
  The full launch to monetize to grow arc, a cold quiz, and flashcards.

## What you will be able to do by Sunday

- Run a PMF survey that tells you something true, and segment it the way
  Superhuman did instead of chasing the aggregate number.
- Build a lifecycle re-engagement sequence that wins users back at a fraction of
  acquisition cost, without crossing the FTC's dark-pattern line.
- Identify your product's natural growth loop and instrument its efficiency.
- Do the k-factor and cycle-time math well enough to tell your investors, or
  yourself, whether a referral program will actually move the needle.
- Assemble it into a single growth system with honest projections, and know when
  to stop optimizing and go back to the product.

Growth without retention is a leaky bucket. Everything this week bends toward the
same discipline you learned to measure in Block 5: keep the users you win, and
make the ones you keep help you win the next ones.
