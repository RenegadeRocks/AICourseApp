# Week 18 — The Demand-Capture Machine

Block 6 got you launched and monetizing. Week 18 builds the machine that turns
strangers into a repeatable stream of leads: a lead magnet worth an email, an
opt-in funnel that stays deliverable, an organic distribution flywheel, and a
paid-ads test that gathers data instead of setting money on fire.

The through-line this week: **a lead is only worth capturing if you have a place
to put it and a next step to send it to.** Most founders build a magnet with no
funnel, or a funnel with no magnet, or run ads into either one before the unit
economics say they should. You will build all three in the right order, and
pre-register the kill/scale threshold before you spend a rupee on ads.

## What you will own by Sunday

1. **One AI-native lead magnet** built on your existing product (Mon, Sat).
2. **An opt-in page + welcome sequence** that passes the 2026 deliverability
   bar (Tue, Sat).
3. **A two-week organic distribution plan** repurposing one idea across
   platforms (Wed, Sat).
4. **A minimum-viable paid-ads test** with a pre-registered CAC target and a
   written kill/scale rule (Thu, Fri, Sat).
5. **A funnel-metrics calculator** (`code-lab/1`) that computes opt-in rate,
   CPL, CAC, funnel conversion, and the paid-test go/no-go decision.
