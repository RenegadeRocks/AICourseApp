# Week 20 — Make the business run without you, then give it a home

You have the front of the machine (lead magnets and paid ads, Week 18) and the
middle (client dashboards and a productized service, Week 19). What you do not
yet have is the part that lets the whole thing run when you are asleep, on a
plane, or serving three clients at once: the written system. This week you build
two things that compound long after you build them. First, the SOP layer, the
operating system that turns what lives in your head into a process anyone (or any
agent) can run. Second, the community layer, the paid inner circle that turns a
list of customers into a place they belong, which is the one asset AI cannot
clone overnight.

This is the last content week of Block 7. By Saturday you will own a runnable SOP
library and a community plan a stranger could join. By Sunday you will have the
whole Block 7 arc, demand capture to delivery infrastructure to systematized
growth, in one piece.

## The seven days

- **[[01-mon-sops-the-operating-system-of-a-scaling-business|Mon]] · SOPs, the
  operating system.** What an SOP is and is not, the SOP hierarchy, checklists
  that beat prose (Gawande), the AI angle (generated and executed SOPs), and when
  NOT to systematize.
- **[[02-tue-onboarding-sops-the-first-30-days|Tue]] · Onboarding SOPs.** The
  first 30 days decide retention. Time-to-first-value, the onboarding checklist,
  automating welcome and setup, and measuring activation.
- **[[03-wed-delivery-and-growth-sops|Wed]] · Delivery & growth SOPs.** The
  productized-service runbook, quality gates, the growth SOP library, and the
  SOP-to-agent pipeline: which procedures graduate into automation.
- **[[04-thu-building-community-the-compounding-moat|Thu]] · Community as moat.**
  Why belonging is the durable moat in the AI era, free vs paid, platform choice
  (Skool/Circle/Discord/Slack), the cold-start problem, and seeding culture.
- **[[05-fri-the-paid-inner-circle-and-membership-economics|Fri]] · The paid
  inner circle.** Designing the membership, tier and pricing design, membership
  unit economics (MRR, churn, LTV), and avoiding the ghost-town death.
- **[[06-sat-build-sop-library-and-community-launch|Sat]] · BUILD.** Three core
  SOPs, the ones that become agents, and a full paid-community launch plan.
  Ships with a `code-lab/` SOP-template generator and a membership
  economics/retention model.
- **[[07-sun-synthesis-quiz-flashcards|Sun]] · Synthesis + Block 7 capstone.**
  Lead-gen to delivery infra to SOPs and community, a cold quiz, and flashcards.

## What you will be able to do by Sunday

- Write an SOP a stranger could execute, in checklist form, with an owner, a
  version, a trigger, and a flag for whether it should become an agent.
- Map your onboarding to time-to-first-value and cut the friction that kills
  month-12 retention.
- Choose a community platform on economics, not hype, and design a paid inner
  circle with tiers you can defend.
- Model MRR, churn, and LTV for a membership, and read the ghost-town
  early-warning metric before the community dies.
- Assemble a runnable SOP library and a launch plan for the first 30 members.

A business that lives in your head is a job with extra steps. A business written
down as SOPs and held together by a community is an asset. This week you build
the asset.
