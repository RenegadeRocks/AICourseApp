# Week 22 — Final Thoughts, Recap & Checklists

> The last week of 26. Not new material. The week where the whole course
> becomes one thing you can hold in your hand: a map, a checklist, a set of
> principles that outlast the tools, a re-verification habit, and a dated plan
> for your next 90 days.

## What this week is (and is not)

This is the program capstone. It re-teaches nothing. Every idea already has a
canonical home somewhere in Blocks 0–7, and this week's job is to link them
into a single operating system you can run a business on. If a day here explains
a concept from scratch, it has failed. Instead each day pulls the through-lines
tight and hands you something durable.

It is also the week the course hands you its own secret skill. Everything you
learned has a shelf life. Model names, prices, and tool features decay in
months — this very course was refreshed in July 2026 because three-month-old
content was already wrong in places. The frameworks did not decay. Thursday
teaches you to tell the difference and to keep the knowledge alive yourself.

## How to run this week

Read Monday to Wednesday as recall practice: at each wikilink, try to restate
the linked idea in one sentence before you follow it. If you cannot, that is the
topic to revisit. Thursday and Friday are forward-facing — do the exercises with
your real business, not a hypothetical one. Saturday you build the artifact.
Sunday you take the final quiz cold, then read the send-off.

The pass bar for the whole week: you finish with a dated, personalized action
plan and a self-audit score you would be willing to show a mentor.

## Prerequisites

The entire program. This week assumes you have working knowledge of all eight
blocks. If a wikilink target is unfamiliar, that is a signal to reread it, not a
reason to slow down here.
